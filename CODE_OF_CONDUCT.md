# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive community. Participation must remain free from harassment, intimidation, discrimination, and personal attacks.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence, not personal characteristics.
- Give actionable feedback with appropriate technical context.
- Respect different experience levels, backgrounds, and communication styles.
- Acknowledge mistakes, correct them, and avoid escalating disagreements.
- Protect security reports, personal information, and other sensitive material.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Discriminatory or derogatory language or imagery.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberate disruption, sustained hostility, or retaliation against reporters.
- Using project spaces to promote illegal or abusive activity.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban participants when behavior conflicts with this policy. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid conflicts of interest where practical.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's [private vulnerability reporting form](https://github.com/Noetance-Labs/corrobore/security/advisories/new). Prefix the report title with `Code of Conduct`. Do not include sensitive conduct reports in public issues or discussions.

Maintainers will acknowledge reports as soon as practical, preserve confidentiality to the extent possible, and communicate the outcome to the reporter. Good-faith reports must not result in retaliation.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone represents the project in public or private project-related communication.
